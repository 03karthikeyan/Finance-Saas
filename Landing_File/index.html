Finance Industry Challenges

Managing a Finance Business
Shouldn't Be Complicated.

Finance and micro-lending operations struggle every day with disconnected tools, lost records, and untracked field collections.

Manual Collection Tracking

Relying on physical paper notebooks and registers leads to lost entries, ink smudges, unrecorded cash, and delayed auditing.

Difficult Borrower Management

Keeping track of borrower KYC, guarantor details, active loans, and payment history across different folders is disorganized and chaotic.

Multiple Branch Confusion

Branch managers operating in silos make it impossible for company owners to know consolidated cash in hand or actual daily recovery figures.

Agent Collection Tracking Problems

No real-time way to verify if field collection agents actually visited borrowers, collected payments, or are carrying un-deposited cash.

Loan Calculation Mistakes

Manual calculation of daily interest, penalty charges, processing fees, and odd-day installments leads to costly revenue leakage.

Pending Due & Defaulter Tracking

Without automated alerts, overdue installments pile up unnoticed until bad debts create severe working capital crisis.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know about implementing FinanceMaster Pro for your daily, weekly, and monthly lending operations.

Can FinanceMaster Pro handle both Daily and Weekly loans simultaneously?
▼
Yes, absolutely. FinanceMaster Pro is specifically architected to manage daily route collections (e.g. 100 days), weekly micro-finance batches, and monthly EMI loans under one centralized database with separate route ledgers and dedicated reporting.
Can field agents collect payments without active internet in rural routes?
▼
Yes! The Agent mobile app includes an Offline Mode that records payments, issues offline thermal/SMS receipts, and automatically synchronizes all records back to the central server the moment network connectivity resumes.
How does the system prevent agent cash theft or reconciliation errors?
▼
Every transaction triggers an immediate SMS confirmation to the borrower's registered mobile number. At the end of each day, the branch manager conducts a digital reconciliation scan that matches cash in hand against recorded receipts before clearing the agent's route.
Can we import our existing borrower records from Excel or physical books?
▼
Yes. Our onboarding specialists provide turnkey CSV/Excel migration tools and assistance to transfer your borrower master records, loan histories, and outstanding balance schedules in less than 24 hours.
Can branch managers only see their own branch data?
▼
Yes, strict branch-level isolation ensures managers and staff only view data belonging to their assigned territory, while the Central Company Admin enjoys complete top-level visibility across all branches.
